Why does this Matter?

Visualizing haunted place data helps identify patterns and trends in paranormal sightings across time and geography. By mapping apparitions, witness counts, and location data, these visualizations provide valuable insights into the cultural, social, and regional significance of hauntings. They allow researchers and enthusiasts to explore correlations, detect anomalies, and engage with the data in a more meaningful way, offering a unique perspective on the unexplained phenomena and their impact on different communities.
